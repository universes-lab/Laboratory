Rules drawn

  1. Each ring passes through the point hole of its torus along the torus axis.
  2. Tori never overlap; neighbouring tori touch.
  3. One torus for quantron and latcher: horn, round tube, outer radius R (a = b = R/2). Its area is π²R², π/4 of the sphere's: the same 2D held in a smaller, denser surface.
  4. Ring length equals the equator, 2πR. Ring shape: planar ellipse.
  5. Two rings sharing a hole move together there.
