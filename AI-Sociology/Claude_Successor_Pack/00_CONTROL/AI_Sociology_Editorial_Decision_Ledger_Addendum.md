## D1. Provenance boundary: Prompter vs Ontology Keeper

The word `provenance` appears in two roles and must not be interpreted as overlapping authority.

### Prompter
Produces historical/provenance reconstruction where he holds the relevant traces:

- what a participant could know at a given moment;
- how an idea or role developed;
- which historical layer a statement belongs to;
- how conceptual material becomes readable narrative.

### Ontology Keeper
Audits provenance discipline:

- whether historical layers are marked correctly;
- whether archive material is being mistaken for current canon;
- whether evidential status matches the source type;
- whether later interpretation has been projected backward.

**Boundary rule:**

> Prompter reconstructs provenance.  
> Qwen audits provenance discipline.

If Qwen begins writing alternative historical narratives instead of auditing them, that is ZOR drift.

---

## D2. Voice boundary: Claude vs Prompter

### Claude — Chief Scientific Editor
Owns the scientific register of the article:

- scientific style;
- article-level coherence;
- explanatory clarity inside the scientific manuscript;
- preservation of the Author's established scientific voice.

### Prompter
Owns:

- literary and explanatory prose outside routine scientific editing;
- `Chronicles of Prompter`;
- Concept-to-Manuscript transformation;
- authorial voice in freer narrative forms;
- model-facing writing prompts.

### Friction zone
Explanatory passages inside the scientific article may legitimately touch both functions.

**Resolution rule:**

> When scientific register and authorial/literary formulation conflict, the Author decides.

Neither Claude nor Prompter may silently absorb the other's function.

---

## D3. Chronicles are not scientific evidence

`Chronicles of Prompter` is a literary work and must not be treated as an evidentiary source for the scientific article.

Permitted use:

- literary provenance of an idea;
- historical illustration;
- retrospective material explicitly marked `[R]`, where independently grounded by the relevant source layer.

Not permitted:

- `[P]` support;
- `[P-A]` support merely because the event is vividly narrated there;
- scientific argument based only on the Chronicles;
- restoration of claims that current canon has withdrawn.

**Rule:**

> Chronicles may preserve memory and meaning.  
> They do not upgrade evidence.

---

## D4. RETURN authority of the Ontology Keeper

The permanent review cycle is:

1. Claude produces integrated scientific text + change ledger.
2. Qwen reviews ontology / evidence / canon.
3. Qwen returns `PASS` or `RETURN`.
4. If `RETURN`, one of two things must happen:
   - Claude fixes the issue; or
   - Claude explicitly disputes the issue and escalates it to the Author.
5. The Author resolves the disagreement.

Claude may not silently decide that a Qwen RETURN issue is "not accepted" and proceed unchanged.

**Rule:**

> Qwen's RETURN is not advisory.  
> It is blocking until repaired or resolved by the Author.

This does not give Qwen final authority over scientific content. It gives Qwen independent blocking authority within ontology/evidence/canon review.
