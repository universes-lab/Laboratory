## 0. PURPOSE

This document is **not a rewrite of the theory** and **not a new canon**. Its function is to control migration from the present mixed v.1/v1.5 repository state toward a coherent HONC canon by separating five things that must not be collapsed:

1. **MIGRATION LOCK** — what may not be carried forward unchanged from v.1;
2. **CONTINUITY CORE** — what survives most strongly;
3. **CANON CANDIDATES** — what is mature enough for the nearest decision triads;
4. **TRIAD QUEUE** — the ordered content-decision rounds;
5. **RESEARCH BACKLOG** — work that becomes meaningful only after upstream decisions.

> **No substantive theory decision is made by this document.**  
> The Methodologist organizes sequence, dependencies and migration safety. Canon decisions belong to the current triad or to a triad assembled specifically for the question.

The principal result of the gray-zone discussion is a **category migration**:

> **v.1 tendency:** the quantron is an elementary non-empty building block of matter.  
> **HONC direction:** the spatial quantum is the elementary carrier; matter is a state/property of space; the quantron becomes an emergent extended temporal/topological pattern over many quanta.

# I. MIGRATION LOCK

`MIGRATION LOCK` marks identities, explanations and formulas that **must not be copied unchanged from v.1**. Locking does not mean automatic deletion; it means mandatory re-attestation.

### ML-01 — `quantron = elementary non-empty quantum`
**LOCK.** New direction separates **quantum** (elementary carrier) from **quantron** (extended pattern). Do not silently overwrite OBJ-010; preserve provenance and supersede/version explicitly.

### ML-02 — `protoquark = one-quantum object`
**LOCK.** Any literal one-cell definition depends on the old quantron identity. If the term survives, redefine it on the new pattern/state basis.

### ML-03 — Latcher motion from “occupies 1.5 quanta”
**LOCK.** Replace transport-of-object logic with propagation/reassignment of a pattern/envelope over stationary substrate quanta.

### ML-04 — direct identity `Q0 = dark matter`
**LOCK.** Base Q0 becomes the ubiquitous 3D substrate/state. The replacement idea “transition-derived/topologically marked Q0-like configuration = dark matter” remains a hypothesis.

### ML-05 — `quantum (quantron)` as synonyms in nesting definitions
**LOCK.** Nesting attaches to the quantum; quantron migrates to pattern scale. Macro/micro-continuum definitions require rewriting.

### ML-06 — old zero-speed statement
**LOCK.** Remove “our zero speed = lower-level c.” Candidate dt/dx replacement:
- `c` → minimum-slowness boundary `s_min = 1/c`;
- `v -> 0` → `dt/dx -> infinity`;
- level transition → scale jump across a barrier, not ordinary deceleration to a shared finite value.

### ML-07 — intermediate proto-object ladder as gray-zone solution
**LOCK.** The discussion weakens the literal ladder and replaces it with generated characteristic scale / envelope / defect / vortex structure.

### ML-08 — one symbol `lambda` for several scales
**LOCK.** Before formula migration distinguish:
1. elementary lattice/quantum spacing;
2. emergent quantron-pattern size;
3. excitation wavelength/characteristic scale;
4. inter-level depth/scale ratio.

### ML-09 — old TD formulas tied to old quantron scale
**LOCK PENDING REDERIVATION.** Any formula depending on old OBJ-010 geometry, old latcher size, or an unresolved bridge to conventional `E, hbar, m, F` must be re-certified.

### ML-10 — “wave/pattern language automatically solves Lorentz invariance”
**LOCK.** Lorentz-compatible behavior must be derived from symmetry/dispersion/slowness structure or another explicit mechanism.

### ML-11 — broad deletion of the gluon-ring layer
**LOCK AGAINST OVER-DELETION.** The rigid assembly geometry is weakened, but color/flavor/axis/antimatter orientation information survives provisionally and must be separated from assembly geometry before deletion.

# II. CONTINUITY CORE

These are the strongest surviving foundations. They are not automatically final canon, but they are the correct stable bridge for the next triads.

### CC-01 — Discrete space
The existence of an elementary spatial quantum survives. Exact numerical scale remains open.

### CC-02 — Multi-component time field
Time remains primary as a multi-component field rather than an external scalar parameter.

### CC-03 — Every spatial quantum is a lower-level continuum/universe
The current repository already contains the core nesting statement. The discussion strengthens it toward explicit infinite nesting and removes quantron-specific ambiguity.

### CC-04 — Generalized Fermat / minimum-time logic
Retained as foundational. Final TD variational form remains to be written.

### CC-05 — Topology as physically meaningful structure
Topology becomes more central: Q-types, defects, latcher/thread stability, winding, transition memory, confinement-like behavior, boundary conditions.

### CC-06 — Axis/orientation logic
Flavor/color/antimatter orientation remains viable even if v.1 global carrier geometry changes.

### CC-07 — Linear/inertial vs rotational temporal components
The distinction survives. Late Author clarification strengthens the linear component as a common carrier/internal ruler for all quanta of a given space.

### CC-08 — `dt/dx` as native TD direction
The program survives and is sharpened into a **slowness** formalism.

### CC-09 — Extended thread/bundle structures
The old constituent ontology changes, but extended latcher/thread structures remain meaningful and may become more natural in a defect/pattern description.

### CC-10 — Classical mass/force/energy are derivative
Conventional quantities remain classical-limit constructs, not primitive ontology. Every use of `E, hbar, m, F` must be identified as native TD variable, derived bridge, or observational translation.

# III. CANON CANDIDATES

Inclusion here means **ready for triad decision**, not “already canon.”

### CCAN-01 — Quantum as the elementary carrier
The elementary object at one spatial level is the **quantum**, not the quantron.  
**Route:** TRIAD A.

### CCAN-02 — Matter as a stratified state/property of space
Matter is not a second primitive substance but a structured state of the same carrier, roughly contrasted with Q0 as unstratified/full 3D.  
**Open:** non-circular geometric/topological definition of stratification.  
**Route:** TRIAD A.

### CCAN-03 — Quantron as an extended pattern
A quantron is an emergent temporal/topological pattern/envelope over many quanta.  
**Open:** exact mathematical identity, stability and scale.  
**Route:** TRIAD A.

### CCAN-04 — Infinite nesting of every quantum
Every quantum is itself a lower-level continuum, with nesting intended as infinite.  
**Correction:** infinite nesting is recursion; what changes is the definition of matter, not the presence of recursion.  
**Route:** TRIAD B.

### CCAN-05 — Base Q0 versus transitional Q0*
- **base Q0:** stable/unstratified substrate state;
- **Q0\*:** transient 3D state inside a transition.  
**Open:** what dynamically/topologically distinguishes them.  
**Route:** TRIAD C.

### CCAN-06 — Common linear temporal carrier
The linear/inertial temporal component is one and the same for all quanta of a given space.  
**Remaining:** object type and formal implementation.  
**Route:** TRIAD A, then formal work.

### CCAN-07 — Slowness as native TD language
`dt/dx` is treated as slowness; slowness surfaces/branches become natural TD objects.  
**Route:** TRIAD A for language/ontology acceptance; formal derivation later.

### CCAN-08 — Vertical and horizontal sectors
- **vertical:** scale/internal multiscale structure;
- **horizontal:** Q-type relationships at one scale.  
**Route:** TRIAD A.

# IV. TRIAD QUEUE

The issue set is too interdependent for seven isolated triads. The transition is organized into four ordered decision rounds.

## TRIAD A — ONTOLOGY & STATE SPACE

### Scope
Resolve the elementary ontology and the mathematical object whose states support the pattern picture.

### Questions
1. What is fundamental at one level?
2. Is matter canonically a stratified state/property of space?
3. What is a quantron after the category migration?
4. What exactly is “stratification”?
5. What canonical status has the common linear temporal carrier?
6. What is state space `M`?
7. How are Q-types represented in `M`?
8. Can sphere/torus and Q-type asymmetries be derived?
9. What is the formal meaning of “projection” from local quantum type to extended pattern?
10. Are vertical/horizontal sectors adopted?

### Deliverables
- approved elementary ontology;
- versioned definitions for quantum, matter, quantron;
- formal task definition for `M`;
- migration decision for Q-type taxonomy;
- v.1 → HONC terminology map;
- decision on what survives from protoquark/latcher/gluon language.

### STOP
Do not decide dark matter, arrow of time, boundary cosmology, or final carrier geometry unless strictly required.

---

## TRIAD B — NESTEDNESS & INTER-LEVEL RELATION

### Scope
Define what infinite nesting means physically and informationally.

### Questions
1. Is nesting explicitly infinite?
2. Does the recursion require a fixed-point formulation?
3. What can level `n` observe about level `n-1`?
4. Is information screening/non-additivity required?
5. How are finite entropy/state-count claims preserved or replaced?
6. Distinguish internal ruler, quantum scale, pattern scale and inter-level depth.
7. Does `alpha` have a derived role in inter-level depth?
8. Which scale relations survive without circular use of conventional bridges?

### Deliverables
- inter-level information law or explicit open hypothesis;
- accepted scale notation;
- status decision on `alpha`-depth;
- recursive/fixed-point formulation task;
- entropy/state-count migration note.

### STOP
No empirical scale claim is promoted solely because of numerical matching.
