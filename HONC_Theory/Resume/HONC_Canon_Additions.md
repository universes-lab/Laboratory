## A. Ontology and terminology
- Q0–Q3 are topological types of the space quantum (λ); letter Q shared by quantum and quantron; scale carried by a label. [dt/dx][C]
- "Quantron" and "latcher" are pattern-scale terms only; at quantum level only Q-types. [dt/dx][C]
- Matter is a stratified state of space, not a second substance. [dt/dx][C]
- Nesting is infinite in both directions; every quantum is a lower-level continuum. [dt/dx][C]
- Time is continuous; τ₀ is a characteristic scale of a level, not a quantum. Lower-level time exists but is unmeasurable from our level (π image). [dt/dx][C]
- dt/dx is slowness, a primary quantity, not inverse velocity. [dt/dx][C]
- c is the minimum-slowness barrier 1/c; v = 0 is the infinite-slowness barrier. [dt/dx][C]
- Arrow of time: cascade t→b→c→s→u via Q3; regeneration u→t via Q0 (Big Bang). [dt/dx][C]
- Base Q0 (stable substrate) and transitional Q0* are distinct. [dt/dx][C]

## B. Components of the time field
- Three components: linear, rotational, double (1D+2D). Pure Q0 has no third. [dt/dx][C]
- Linear: first derivative of time over space is constant. Rotational: second derivative. [dt/dx][H]
- Rotational component is the gradient of slowness. [dt/dx][H]
- Linear component is one for the whole space — a common ruler. [dt/dx][D]
- Third component = projection of the rotational budget onto edge (1D) and face (2D) by the cube-diagonal rule; magnitude continuous, orientation discrete (source of L). [dt/dx][H]
- Gravity is isotropic from the quantron level: axes of constituent quanta average out. [dt/dx][C]
- Toroidal contributions do not average: the torus axis is fixed by docking. Hence electromagnetism is directional. [dt/dx][D]
- Q3 instability comes from its internal Lorentz component (three perpendicular axes). [dt/dx][C]
- Q3 has no rotation axis as a whole; its rings rotate. Reference of zero rotational share. [dt/dx][H]

## C. Budget
- Components share one budget and add in quadrature (cube diagonal: 1/3 + 2/3 = 1). [dt/dx][C]
- Translation and rotation of Q0 share a budget; the equator binds. Valid for two-component Q0. [dt/dx][D]
- Q0 translates along its own axis, pole first. [dt/dx][D]
- Photon chain does not rotate about its axis of motion; neutrino does and is slower. [dt/dx][D]
- Ring and torus speeds of a photon are projections of one budget, not velocities added to translation. [dt/dx][H]
- Only the second (rotational) component competes with translation; the third does not. [dt/dx][H]
- Range principle: the edge of the budget is unreachable; quanta of one type differ within a range. [dt/dx][C]

## D. Gravity
- Cells are uniform in number; gradient of slowness comes from the distribution of I by Q-type. [dt/dx][H]
- Solid Q0 creates no local gradient. [dt/dx][H]
- ∇²s = −k·ρ_I (k > 0): potential is s, gravity is ∇s. [dt/dx][H]
- Outside matter: s = s∞ + A/r, ∇s ∝ 1/r², A > 0 because rotation costs time. [dt/dx][H]
- Field outside depends only on total excess I (sphere theorem). [dt/dx][D]
- ρ_I is built from the trace of the inertia tensor. [dt/dx][D]
- Fermat = minimum of total time; path bends toward larger s. [dt/dx][D]
- Ray equation d/dl(s·dr/dl) = ∇s for light — direction only. [bridge][H]
- Photon is massless because ω = 0 about its axis of motion, not because I = 0. [dt/dx][C]
- Photon cells carry I ≠ I₀ and contribute to the slowness gradient. [dt/dx][H]
- Condition on dark matter: it must have I different from the Q0 background. [dt/dx][D]

## E. λ-molecules
- Latcher bond: maximally inflated tori, holes ~two micro-quanta, two rings per hole, each ring through its own and a neighbouring torus, axes perpendicular. [dt/dx][C]
- Contact graph is a cycle; cube graph forbidden. [dt/dx][D]
- Number of links is even. [dt/dx][D]
- Links = 2ⁿ for n axes; full vertex coverage guaranteed only along docked axes. [dt/dx][D]
- Exactly three: Z (n=1), W (n=2), gluon (n=3). No fourth axis in 3D. [dt/dx][D]
- One object — the λ-molecule — parametrised by n. [dt/dx][D]
- Strong = n=3, weak = n≤2; replaces the canonical crystal/scatter distinction removed by Р-4. [dt/dx][H]
- W: only ring axis docked, torus axis free → uncompensated → charge. Z: both docked → full compensation. [dt/dx][C]
- Free torus axis in W and the tilt within the hole's gap are one fact. [dt/dx][D]
- Gluon compensation is a stability selection; Z compensation is geometric necessity. [dt/dx][C]
- λ-molecule is a store of rotation — topologically locked, externally compensated — not a "glue". [dt/dx][H]
- Gluons form no drops of their own (canonical crystals excluded by Р-4). [dt/dx][H]
- Gluons are a medium inside quantrons, visible at the parton level. [dt/dx][H]
- Gluons carry about half the nucleon momentum. [bridge]
- Z and W are discharges of the rotation store. [dt/dx][H]

## F. Assembly from Q0 (conditional on the equatorial rule)

## G. Energy and mass
- Two energies of inverse structure: internal = time per space (native), kinetic = space per time. [dt/dx][D]
- For extended bundles, kinetic uses cross-section, not volume. [dt/dx][D]
- Mass is not the number of quanta (photon: long bundle, zero mass). [dt/dx][D]
- Mass comes from rotation that happens (ω), not from static I. [dt/dx][H]
