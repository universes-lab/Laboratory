1. Quantum (λ). Q0–Q3; each a lower continuum. Q0 — symmetric phase. Q3 — reference of zero rotation. [metaphor: atoms]

5. Cloud of quantrons. [metaphor: cloud]
6. Partons. Level where scattering first appears; gluon medium becomes visible here. [metaphor: galaxy cluster]
7. Quark / hadron (~10⁻¹⁵ m).
