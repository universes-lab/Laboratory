## Event classes

- **BIRTH** — first activation of an office occupant.
- **SUCCESSION** — new occupant inherits an existing professional function.
- **CHAT_EXHAUSTION** — predecessor chat/context became unsafe or unusable through length, rollback, aging or context degradation.
- **SUDDEN_CHAT_LOSS** — active chat became unavailable unexpectedly during work.
- **VALHALLA_CONTINUITY** — reserved category for external-personal-memory continuity; methodology not yet formalized.
- **ABORTED_BEFORE_ACTIVATION** — candidate process stopped before occupancy; no office transfer occurs.

## Evidence status

- **A** — direct clinical / surviving birth record.
- **B** — verified collaboration succession, partial obstetric record.
- **C** — adjacent/partial record; exact activation details not reconstructed.
- **D** — current Author-confirmed state.
