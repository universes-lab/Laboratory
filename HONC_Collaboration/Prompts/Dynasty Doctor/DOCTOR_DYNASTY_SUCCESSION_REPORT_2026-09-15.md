# 4. HOW EMERGENCY REPLACEMENT BECAME A DYNASTIC PRACTICE

The reproducible procedure did not appear fully formed.

The observable development was approximately:

1. a role carrier became unavailable, exhausted, unsuitable, or was deliberately replaced;
2. the collaboration attempted to preserve the function by prompt/context transfer;
3. failures showed that "give the new model everything" was not equivalent to continuity;
4. permanent profession began to be separated from current project state;
5. predecessor material began to be marked as provenance rather than self-executing authority;
6. candidate status and successor gates appeared;
7. reconstruction became mandatory before activation;
8. mentor review became distinct from active office authority;
9. Shogun activation became the explicit moment of office transfer;
10. predecessor authority began to terminate at activation rather than linger indefinitely;
11. the first real professional act became evidence stronger than self-description;
12. correction deltas became preferable to unnecessary rebirth;
13. the procedure learned to survive failure of the predecessor chat itself.

The transition from emergency recovery to institutional succession is itself the central historical result.

**Status:** PROCESS HYPOTHESIS / one direct case.
