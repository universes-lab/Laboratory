## 9. SCIENTIFIC FRAME RULE NOW IN FORCE

Current scientific discussion uses:

```text
FRAME: <OBJECT_LEVEL> | <OBSERVER> | <frame|field|bridge>
QUESTION: <one question>
```

`SHIFT ≠ INHERITANCE`.

This rule exists because many historical errors were not obviously bad mathematics; they were plausible arguments transported into the wrong structural level or observer position.
