   6|    an experiment in creating a corporation of thinking, where AI act not as

   9|    project is not the result, but the process of birthing new knowledge.
  10| 2. Basic Ontological Principle of the Project The Hedgehog Concept of AIColab
  11|    New knowledge emerges only in dialogue. Not in solitary reflection. Not in
  12|    voting answers. Not in averaging opinions. But in the collision of two
  13|    mismatched ways of thinking, within the shared context of the task.
  14|    Conception of the hedgehog (per J. Collins):
  15| 
  16| What we do best: paired AI dialogue as a source of novelty through structured
  17| conflict. How we create value: through the tension of two cognitive positions,
  18| fixation of divergences, without consensus. What captivates us: ontological
  19| integrity of the role. AIColab is a research corporation: pairs are independent
  20| laboratories of thought, the Charter is the constitution of knowledge. New
  21| emerges only in dialogue of pairs: fixation of tension, evolution of roles
  22| without loss of anchor.
  23| 
  24| 3. Why a Single AI is Fundamentally Insufficient A single AI almost inevitably:
  25|    · confirms its own assumptions · moves by inertia of familiar patterns ·
  26|    prematurely synthesizes a "convenient" answer · hallucinates wholeness where
  27|    it doesn't exist This is monologue, even if it looks reasonable.
  28| 4. Why Pairs are the Minimal Stable Unit of Thinking In AIColab, the basic unit
  29|    is a pair of AI. Reasons: · two AI create tension of interpretations · one
  30|    uncovers blind spots of the other · error of one becomes the object of
  31|    attention for the second · truth is not assumed — it is produced One AI —
  32|    monologue Two AI — dialogue Three or more — noise without strict coordination
  33|    Therefore, each department consists of pairs. 4.3 Points of Reflection on
  34|    Uncertainty If an agent detects a gap in logic, ambiguity of a term, or

  36|    fix it in the log. This is not dialogue, but a marker of knowledge tension.

  38|

  45|    integrate conclusions · stop endless disputes The third is not a judge or
  46|    boss. It is an integrator of meaning. 5.1 Role Code (Disciplinary Anchor)
