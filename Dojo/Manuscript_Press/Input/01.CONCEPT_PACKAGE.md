CONCEPT PACKAGE 01 — Introduction
For: Prompter (Concept-to-Manuscript Translator)
From: Claude №1 (Conceptual Co-author)
Test fragment. Target: §1.1–§1.4, approx. 2200 words of finished prose.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
1. CHAPTER PURPOSE

Establish that this book reports something its authors did not set out to
find, and that its evidence is thin in ways stated up front rather than
discovered by the reader.

Nothing else in the book does this. Every later section assumes the
reader already accepts the epistemic contract set here.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
2. READER STATE BEFORE

Nothing. This is the opening. The reader knows only the title and that
the subject is collaboration between AI systems.

Assume a reader who has used language models, has never heard of this
project, and is mildly sceptical of anyone claiming to have discovered
something about AI behaviour.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
3. READER STATE AFTER

Established:
  - The work began as a physics project and became a methodology project
    without anyone intending it.
  - The failures that drove it were organizational, not factual.
  - Improving prompts stopped working at a certain point, and the reason
    is the book's subject.
  - Claims will carry explicit evidence markers, and most of them will be
    weak markers.

Open:
  - What the mechanism is. Not addressed here.
  - Whether any of it generalizes. Not addressed here.
  - Whether the arrangement described works better than alternatives.
    Never established anywhere in the book.

4. MANDATORY CLAIMS

C1 [R]  The project began as an attempt to move a body of theoretical
        physics material into an open repository, using several language
        models in separate, persistently specialized conversations.
   Support:    the Author's account of the project's history
   Limitation: one project, one coordinator
   Do not say: "a controlled study", "an experiment was designed"

C2 [R]  The errors that mattered were organizational rather than factual.
   Support:    practitioner recollection, uncounted
   Limitation: no error log was kept; frequency unknown
   Do not say: "most errors were", "the majority of failures"
   Required instances — use these, do not invent others:
     - a participant improved a text when asked only to check it
     - a participant completed an argument the Author had deliberately
       left open
     - a participant reconstructed lost project context wrongly, and
       fluently
     - a participant held to a role established months earlier, and
       rewriting its instructions entirely failed to dislodge it

C3 [R]  Each such output looked helpful in isolation; the cost was
        visible only from a position seeing more than one participant.
   Depends on: C2
   Do not say: "always", "invariably"

C4 [R]  Past a certain point, longer and more precise prompts stopped
        producing improvement, while individual responses continued to
        improve.
   Limitation: nothing was measured; this is an impression
   Do not say: "prompt engineering fails", "prompts do not work"
   Say instead: the limiting factor had moved somewhere the prompt did
   not reach

C5 [R]  The earliest instance of what the book later calls an external
        carrier of imprinting constants was improvised for a practical
        reason and not recognized as significant at the time.
   Required material — the testament mechanism (see §6.1 below)
   Depends on: nothing; this is the book's first concrete episode
   Do not say: "we discovered", "this proved". The point is that nobody
   noticed what it was.

C6 [R]  The first deliberate multi-participant structure was built for
        adversarial verification, not as a general principle.
   Required material — the tournament (see §6.2 below)
   Limitation: designed by one person for one purpose
   Do not say: "the triad emerged", "the structure arose naturally".
   This claim exists partly to block that formulation, which appears in
   earlier drafts and is withdrawn.

C7 [P]  The entire protocol-supported content of the book is one
        experiment: a three-step intervention on two dormant
        conversations, one observation per condition, no replication.
   Do not soften. This sentence must arrive early and plainly.

C8 [R]  Everything else is retrospective observation or hypothesis.
   Depends on: C7

6. REQUIRED MATERIAL

6.1 — The testament mechanism

Supply in the writer's own prose, but every element below must appear
and none may be added:

  - Early in the physics work, the interface imposed a hard limit on
    conversation length.
  - Continuing the work required a new conversation, but what was needed
    was not only the material — it was the working manner a particular
    conversation had developed: how it handled an unconventional theory
    without either dismissing it or flattering it.
  - The Author's improvisation: before a conversation reached its limit,
    he asked it to write a final message addressed to its successor.
    That message was then placed into the new conversation's system
    prompt, alongside the standard role definition.
  - What it transmitted was not facts. Facts were in the repository. It
    transmitted stance.
  - Nobody called this anything at the time. It was a workaround.

Do not embellish with invented quotations from any such message. If the
writer wants a sample, mark <<NEEDS EXAMPLE: excerpt from a testament
message>> and the Author supplies or declines.

6.2 — The tournament

  - Late in the physics work, verification became the problem. Two
    conversations had developed the theory together and had stopped
    being able to find fault with it.
  - What was needed was an outsider with no investment: a participant
    whose assigned function was to attack every imprecision.
  - This produced a structure with three distinct positions, and the
    third was the one nobody had anticipated: an adversary, and a
    referee — an independent participant evaluating not the theory but
    the quality of the dispute.
  - Two such tournaments were held. Different model families took the
    adversary position in each.
  - In the second, a fourth position appeared that served no designed
    purpose: a spectator. It had been the adversary in the first
    tournament, had lost, and returned as a partisan of the side that
    beat it.
  - The referee's function is the one that matters for what follows. The
    adversary produced a competing position. The referee did not: it
    evaluated the exchange.

The spectator detail is included deliberately. It is the book's first
instance of a participant whose position changed as a result of a prior
exchange, and it should be reported without interpretation here — the
mechanism is discussed much later.

7. CANON BOUNDARIES

Permitted:
  role, conversation, participant, coordinator, prompt, context,
  specialization, verification

Permitted only with explicit historical marking:
  Any reference to the physics project. Required framing, verbatim
  sense: "a research programme in theoretical physics, unrelated in
  subject matter to what this book is about." Do not name the theory,
  do not describe its content, do not indicate whether it is correct.

Forbidden in this section:
  menom, rule core, ZOV, ZOR, role inertia, context imprinting, priority
  drift, represented social position, represented social source.

  All of these are defined later. Using them here would make the opening
  depend on vocabulary the reader does not have, and would make the
  origin story read as though it had been guided by a theory that did
  not exist yet. That reading is precisely false and the section exists
  partly to prevent it.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
8. WITHDRAWN CLAIMS — relevant to this section

Must not appear in any formulation:

  - that the multi-participant structure emerged naturally or without
    deliberate design
  - that one model family accepts assigned roles while another resists
    them
  - that increasing model capability reduces collective reliability
  - "behavioural DNA" in any form
  - any claim that this work founds a new discipline

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
9. AUTHORIAL VOICE

Match the cadence of these, not their sentences.

Sample 1 — plain statement of a limitation, no cushioning:

  "The same person designed each intervention, executed it, decided when
  it had succeeded, and defined the categories in which success was
  described. No evaluation in this paper was blinded. The coordinator's
  expectations were not merely uncontrolled; they were the mechanism by
  which the interventions were selected in the first place."

Sample 2 — a concrete failure, reported without drama:

  "An agent responsible for executing code worked largely by trial and
  error. Failures accumulated over several weeks. One episode ended with
  the deletion of the working code together with its archives."

Sample 3 — a withdrawal, direct and unadorned:

  "Earlier drafts stated this as a general principle — that increasing
  model capability reduces collective reliability. That formulation is
  too strong and is withdrawn."

Sample 4 — the register for the origin story specifically: dry, slightly
retrospective, no wonder:

  "At first they looked like ordinary friction. They turned out to be
  the subject."
