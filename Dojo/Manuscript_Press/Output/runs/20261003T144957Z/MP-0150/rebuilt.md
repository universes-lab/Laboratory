**[P-A] — Protocol-supported, ancillary** signifies a claim derived from documented protocols, specifically instances of observed behavior explicitly recorded in transcripts within Appendix A. These transcripts encompass verbatim exchanges, intervention sequences, model specifications, interface configurations, and unaltered participant responses. While the evidence itself is inspectable, the specific conditions surrounding the observed behavior were not predetermined nor subject to a formalized control condition. The relevance of these ancillary observations was recognized retrospectively. This categorization denotes a strength level stronger than mere reasoned relevance ([RtagHelperRunner]) because the primary source material allows terceros to corroborate or contest the interpretation. However, it is considered weaker than a fully protocol-defined outcome ([PtagHelperRunner]) due to the absence of predefined experimental controls. Notably, when an ancillary observation serves as a practical near-control for a designed condition, this relationship is explicitly described in prose rather than assigned a distinct label.
