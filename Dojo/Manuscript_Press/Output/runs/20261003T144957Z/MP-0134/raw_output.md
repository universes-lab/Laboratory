Rahwan, Cebrian, Obradovich, and colleagues, in their 2019 *Nature* publication titled "*Machine behaviourastéroïdes*," empirically investigate algorithmic systems through the lens of behavioural science. Their framework employs two key dimensions: the level of analysis (individual machine behaviour, collective machine behaviour, or hybrid human-machine behaviour) and Tinbergen's four questions, a cornerstone of ethological inquiry. **Crucially, this work does not propose a novel discipline.** Instead, its insights specifically pertain to the collective and hybrid levels of analysis within this established behavioural framework.  While earlier drafts suggested the term "AI Ethology," this proposal is retracted as the domain is already conceptually addressed and the individual-level analysis is not the paper's focus.
