Before outlining the specific phenomena described in this paper, it's crucial to state our epistemic stance. These emergent conditions were not part of a priori designed experiments. Rather, we encountered them incidentally during unrelated work. Consequently, our objective is not to definitively prove these phenomena's novelty within the existing literature (a broad, unsubstantiated claim beyond our capacity to verify), but rather to accurately document their occurrence and our characterization of them. Therefore, this section situates our findings within relevant prior work, highlighting points of divergence rather than asserting a priori desconocimiento.
