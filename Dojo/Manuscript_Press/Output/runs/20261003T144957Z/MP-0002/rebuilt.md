The foundational objective of this undertaking was to systematically transfer a substantial corpus of theoretical materials — encompassing books, working notes, and unfinished derivations within the realm of theoretical physics — into an accessible open repository. To achieve this, the strategy entailed employing several distinct language models in parallel, specialized conversations designed to handle specific aspects of the theoretical content. These models functioned as assistants, aiding in the methodical migration of the theoretical subject matter itself.
