BEGIN_CONTINUITY_CACHE
This manuscript offers three distinct elements, delineated in decreasing order of confidence:

1. **A procedure**: A method for returning a conversation to a state preceding a conflict (§10.5). This procedure isolates the influence of a novel prompt from any retroactive impact of the model's prior objections. Notably, its utility subsists irrespective of the paper's broader substantive claims holding or not. 

2. **A countable rule**: A mechanism to minimize unverifiable self-assertions inherent in role-prompt formulations, coupled with a defined falsification pathway (§9.3, §11.4).

3. **A scale and a question**: Introduced in §1.6, this element proposes a structured framework with two substantiated levels, one currently open, and one avenue yet to be empirically tested (§11). Experiments outlined in §11 hold the key to resolving the standing uncertainties regarding the remaining two levels.
END_CONTINUITY_CACHE

BEGIN_CURRENT_SOURCE
Two reasons.

The failures are informative. The experiment in §5 became useful precisely because its first two interventions did not work as intended. Had only the successful third step been recorded, the paper would have concluded that method-preserving prompts work — without the evidence showing why, and without the confounds showing the conclusion is not yet available (§11.8).

And the setting is hard to construct deliberately. Dormant, heavily specialized, months-old conversations inside a live research project are not something a laboratory study easily produces. The conditions were poor for controlled inference and unusually good for noticing what to test.
END_CURRENT_SOURCE
