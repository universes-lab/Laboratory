This paper abandons the term "behavioural DNA," previously employed to conceptualize the underlying mechanisms governing observed behaviors. Its withdrawal stems from an inherent conceptual contradiction. The metaphor of "replicationtagHelperRunner, implying rules directly copying themselves and transmitting unchanged, clashes with the process this work elucidates: development. Development, as described here, posits rules generating a structured output (phenotype) not explicitly present within the rules themselves (akin to genotype-to-phenotype mapping, not genotype-to-genotype). Consequently, retaining "behavioural DNA" necessitated continual disclaimers regarding biological inheritance, signifying its ineffectiveness in accurately representing the core argument.
