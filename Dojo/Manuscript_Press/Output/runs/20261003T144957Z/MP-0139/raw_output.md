Prior multi-agent literature primarily exemplifies condition 1 of the taxonomy delineated in §4.5, involving direct inter-model output exchange. Conversely, this paper uniquely focuses on condition 4, where prompts describe model interactions and positional relationships without explicit output transfer. The experimental manipulation centers on varying these positional descriptions within geïsoleted conversations. Notably, a single ancillary observation touches upon condition 2 (human-relayed attributed transfer) albeit impurely, as acknowledged in §5.5. Condition 3 (human-relayed unattributed transfer) is not empirically addressed in this study.
