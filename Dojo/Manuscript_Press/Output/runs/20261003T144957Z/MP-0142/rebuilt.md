We decline to provide a definitive table of platform capabilities. Such a table would be inherently time-bound, accurate only on the date of its creation and subject to change due to the evolving nature of these functionalities. The paper's focus centers on the demonstrable variance and dynamism of these capabilities across platforms, their influence on dormant conversation resumption contexts, and the historical lack of such documentation at the time of the reported observations. Therefore, a static table would not accurately reflect the pertinent aspects relevant to the argument.
