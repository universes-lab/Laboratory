Four distinct conditions should be delineated, as defined in §4.5:

1. **Direct inter-model interaction:** One model receives the output of another model through a designated communication channel.
2. **Human-relayed attributed transfer:** A human mediates the transfer of an output while explicitly stating its originating model.
3. **Human-relayed unattributed transfer:** The human intermediary transmits an output without disclosing the source model.
4. **Represented structure without transfer:** The prompt describes the involvement of other models and their relationships, yet no explicit output is transferred between them.
