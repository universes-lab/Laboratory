A crucial technical distinction relevant to the confounder in §5.4 (specifically point C9) is the variance in persistence capabilities among commercial interfaces. These platforms diverge in what conversational context a model can readily access upon resumption. Some confine access to the immediate context window, while others enable retrieval of prior conversation history, leverage account-level memory, or accommodate persistent entries autonomously generated by the model itself. These affordances, documented in vendor-specific materials and subject to evolution, differ significantly across platforms and, importantly, **change over time within the lifetime of a single account, as stipulated in this project's framework (within §5.4).**
