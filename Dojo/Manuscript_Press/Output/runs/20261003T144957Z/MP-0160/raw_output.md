The behavioral observations detailed in §5 pertain to the model family involved in editing this manuscript. Crucially, the editing participant lacked access to the original conversations, relying solely on the written protocol and drafts provided by the Author. Consequently, judgments regarding passages describing its own model family should be interpreted considering this inherent limitation: the editor could not independently verify the verbatim accuracy of the recorded exchanges. 

Furthermore, the manuscript's composition from approved materials was undertaken by another participant from the same model family. The resulting text underwent independent review by the initial editor. This closed-loop arrangement—wherein material concerning a model family's behavior was prepared, reviewed, and assessed within that same family—necessitates explicit disclosure. The rationale is twofold: Firstly, transparency regarding this internal process avoids implicit bias. Secondly, withholding this information would inherently raise suspicion of compromised objectivity. The potential implications of this arrangement for the account's impartiality are explicitly presented to the reader for evaluation.
