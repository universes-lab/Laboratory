This paper offers distinct reading paths tailored to different reader objectives. A reader primarily focused on the empirical evidence should consult sections §3 (defining evidential labels and failure modes), §5 (presenting the core experimental account), and §12.7 (summarizing limitations and reflecting on the evidence base). Researchers looking to directly apply the methodology outlined should delve into sections §9 and §10.5, which detail the methodological framework and its practical implementation. Conversely, individuals intending to critically evaluate or challenge the proposed framework should begin their exploration with §5.4, where the specific conceptual underpinnings and potential points of contention are addressed.
