The complete evidence base for this paper consists of a singular experiment: a three-step role-reconfiguration intervention applied to two dormant conversations from two distinct model families, executed within a single research project by one human operator. This experiment yielded one controlled observation per condition. Furthermore, four extraneous observations exist: preserved transcripts of unspecified conditions, lacking structured experimental arrangement. These are denoted as ** EVIDENCE BASE: ONE EXPERIMENT and FOUR UNSTRUCTURED OBSERVATIONS (P-A)**.
