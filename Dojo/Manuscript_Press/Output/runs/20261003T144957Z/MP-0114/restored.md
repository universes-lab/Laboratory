This paper unfolds as follows:

- **§2** situates the work within existing research, delineating which concepts align with established terminology (predominantly aligning).
- **§3** defines the evidential labels and characterizes the recognized failure modes for each conceptual category.
- **§4** establishes the conceptual framework and traces its genesis.
- **§5** presents the cornerstone empirical account: a three-step experiment detailing its outcomes, nine identified structural confounds, and four ancillary observations.
- **§6** logically derives the concepts substantiated by the evidence, ordered according to their scope of implication.
- **§7** documents practitioner observations that inspired the framework yet lack direct evidential support for it. 
- Subsequent sections (§8-§12) elaborate on the structural hypothesis, methodological considerations, experimental design, and concluding limitations and ethical reflections.
