The convention of distinguishing between **Verified** and **Provisional** bibliographic references is crucial due to a specific episode during this section's preparation. Thirteen bibliographic items were sourced from an external participant, resulting in inaccuracies: five were outright incorrect, and two misinterpreted the direction of cited arguments, citing papers supporting a claim as evidence against it. Furthermore, two arXiv identifiers intended for multi-agent-debate literature pointed to unrelated papers in observational astrophysics and cosmology. This instance, detailed in §12.4.1, exemplifies the fallibility of solely relying on intermediary bibliographic sourcing. Consequently, flagging provisional references with a warning symbol (⚠) and mandating independent verification underscores the methodological necessity of heightened scrutiny for such citations.
