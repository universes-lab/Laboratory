Hong and Page (PNAS 101(46), 2004) posit a conditional result: within their model's assumptions, a randomly chosen ensemble of diverse problem solvers can outperform a group consisting solely of the individually highest-performing solvers. However, Thompson (*Notices of the AMSastéroïdes 61(9 незавершена, 2014tagHelperRunner) contends that the application of this argument is fundamentally flawed. Thompson argues that the prevailing interpretation—namely, that diverse, comparatively weaker agents outperform homogeneous, highly capable ones—misrepresents the theorem's core claim. Thompson maintains that neither the theorem itself nor the ensuing debate concerning it substantiates the specific arrangement described in the present context.
