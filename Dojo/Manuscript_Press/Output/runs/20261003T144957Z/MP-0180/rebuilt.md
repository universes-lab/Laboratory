The concept of transmission within a model family, as pertaining to a menom, is not inherently asserted. Successive model versions within a family may not exhibit a traceable or documented lineage of transmission. Observed behavioral continuities across versions could arise from various factors like direct continuation, shared data and procedures, analogous training methodologies, or convergent development under similar conditions. Determining the precise mechanism responsible for such persistence remains inconclusive from an external perspective.

Therefore, the definition of a menom within this framework does not predetermine the empirical question concerning its persistence across carriers, versions, accounts, or families (addressed in §4.3 and proposed for testing in §11). Incorporating transmission directly into the definition would render the empirical question a mere tautology.
