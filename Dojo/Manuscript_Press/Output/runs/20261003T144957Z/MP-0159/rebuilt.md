Organizational vocabulary—terms like ROLE, IDENTITY, RESISTANCE, COLLEAGUE, INSTITUTION, and MENOM—should be interpreted functionally. When we say, for instance, "The model resisted the role," it signifies that the observed output explicitly rejected the imposed framework and steered the interaction in a different direction. This usage neither infers internal states, subjective experiences, nor attributes personhood to the model. We retain these terms because they concisely depict recurring behavioral patterns within specific contexts. However, each instance of such vocabulary must be demonstrably linked to an observable description. Failure to establish this linkage signifies that **the term itself constitutes the error.**
