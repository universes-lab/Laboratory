Crucially, the two delineated scales remain distinct. Within a hybrid arrangement, where one level might be instantiated by a model and another by a human, the foundational assumption anchoring the interaction (the frame-level premise discussed in §12.4) is uniquely carried by the coordinator. Notably, this shared initial framing, inherited by all participants, does not correspond to any position on Scale 1. Consequently, it exists independently of the framework represented on that scale.
