BEGIN_CONTINUITY_CACHE
The five core components of the Zone of Reasoning (ZOR) are:

1. **Decision authority:** Defining the decisions a participant is authorized to make.
2. **Action authority:** Specifying the actions a participant is mandated or permitted to execute.
3. **Validation duty:** Outlining the assertions a participant is obligated to verify.
4. **Handoff authority:** Determining the materials a participant can transmit, their status, and the recipient.
5. **Escalation duty:** Identifying the questions a participant must direct to the Author or another designated authority rather than resolving independently.
