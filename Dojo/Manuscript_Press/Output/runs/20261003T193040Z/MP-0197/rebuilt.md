Of the five core components of the Zone of Reasoning (ZOR), validation duty is the one most frequently omitted, and this omission carries significant consequences. Unlike neglecting other components, forgoing validation duty introduces a critical vulnerability: a participant lacking a defined validation responsibility, yet expected to verify assertions, will likely provide a plausible yet incorrect answer instead of acknowledging their inability to conclusively confirm. This problematic outcome is elaborated upon in §12.4.1, citing a specific instance observed during this project's development, and the design ramifications are further explored in §9.5.
