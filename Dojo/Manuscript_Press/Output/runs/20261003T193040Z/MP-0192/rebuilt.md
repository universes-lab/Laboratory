Two distinct boundaries delineate a participant's operational position, and these must be explicitly defined separately. We henceforth abbreviate them as **ZOR** (Zone of Responsibility) and **ZOV** (Zone of Visibility), consistently employing these terms throughout the subsequent discussion. 


> **ZOV — zone of visibility.** The limits of the information and context available to a participant when forming a response or decision.
>
> **ZOR — zone of responsibility.** The limits of the decisions, actions and handoffs for which the participant is accountable within the process.
