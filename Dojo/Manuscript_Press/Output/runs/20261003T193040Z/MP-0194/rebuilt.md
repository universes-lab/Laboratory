The four components of the Zone of Visibility (ZOV) are:

1. **Context Access:** This delineates the messages, documents, data, and prior decisions accessible to a participant. 2. **Source Visibility:** This specifies whether a participant knows the genuine origin of a message (beyond declared attributions) or only the declared attribution itself. 3. **Relational Visibility:** This component outlines the participant's awareness of the roles, relationships, and interaction histories of other participants involved. 4. **Output Visibility:** This defines which outputs or results generated by others a participant observes prior to formulating their own decisions or actions.
