The Zone of Visibility (ZOV) fundamentally governs the *availability* of information to a participant. Crucially, it does not imply that the participant correctly interprets this accessible information nor that they are authorized to disseminate it. 

Specifically, the experiment described in §5 manipulated relational visibility as its primary factor and source visibility secondarily (§5.5). The manipulation held the other two ZOV components—context access and output visibility—constant.
