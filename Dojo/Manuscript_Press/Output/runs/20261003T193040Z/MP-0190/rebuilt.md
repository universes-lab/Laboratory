A core within this framework can exhibit fixation through two distinct origins, yet the underlying mechanism governing this fixation remains consistent. Firstly, fixation can arise from the foundational architectural, training, alignment procedure, and system configuration established *before any interaction commences*. This pre-interaction setup inherently predisposes the core towards a particular mode of operation, applicable in principle to every instance of that model family. 

Secondly, fixation can occur *during an interaction itself*. A specific early episode, characterized by a particular type of work and operational standards, acts as a fixation point. Subsequent inputs are then interpreted and responded to relative to this established context.
