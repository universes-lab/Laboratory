Methodologically, this distinction is crucial because the experiment in §5 manipulated the *represented* institutional position described to participants, while the participants' *actual* Zone of Reasoning (limited to their context window and unmodified interaction) remained constant. No actual changes in role, access, or communication pathways occurred due to the variation in represented positions. Consequently, any observed model responses to these described structures reflect a reaction to the textual representation, not a genuine enactment of altered social dynamics. This methodological point underscores the limitation of the paper's claims (**§2.9**) to the realm of responding to represented, not enacted, organizational structures. Divergence between these two boundary types was a constant in this study, a variable the experiment itself did not systematically manipulate.
