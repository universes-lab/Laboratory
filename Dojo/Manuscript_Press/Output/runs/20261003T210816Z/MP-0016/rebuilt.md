Within this framework, the variable element was not the inherent structure itself, but rather the **description** of that structure (§4.5). This distinction gives rise to the paper's two central constructs: 1) **represented social position**, a textual description of an agent's place within the organizational framework, provided irrespective of the functional existence of communication channels; and 2) **represented social source**, denoting the ascribed origin and standing of incoming messages, again derived solely from textual descriptions.
