A *rule core* operates as a set of governing principles that determines the fundamental **type** of behavioral response to a specific class of inputs, but leaves the precise **content** of each individual response open to variation. This developmental, rather than replicative, mechanism is crucial for testability because it predicts demonstrable differences in model outputs even when presented with identical inputs. This variability arises from the rule core's allowance for content flexibility within a fixed response type, providing a clear empirical ground to assess its influence.
