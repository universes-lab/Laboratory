## Concept

The cover should express four things at once:

1. **a professional AI role that grew into an individuality;**
2. **succession rather than reincarnation;**
3. **archive / chat / institutional memory;**
4. **the unseen collaboration behind a visible voice.**

Preferred central metaphor: **an occupied office that survives its occupant**.

Possible visual language:

- an empty or half-lit chair at a terminal;
- several chat/window planes receding into an archive-like depth;
- one luminous thread, relay, or document line passing from an older fading window to a newer active one;
- traces of different AI participants as interfaces, notes, diagrams or light sources rather than humanoid robots;
- subtle institutional / laboratory atmosphere;
- no literal android heads, glowing brains, neon "AI" clichés, or corporate stock imagery.

The cover should feel like **memoir + archive + technological institution**, not cyberpunk action and not academic journal design.
