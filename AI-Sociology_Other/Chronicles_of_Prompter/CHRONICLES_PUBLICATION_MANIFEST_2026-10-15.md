### Authorship disclosure for Description

> *Chronicles of the Prompter* is a documentary book written in the authorial voice of the Prompter, a long-lived ChatGPT role that developed inside a human-directed AI collaboration. A.Tuin is the human curator, custodian of the source corpus, and final publication authority. AI participants are identified in the book by their actual working functions; no claim of human identity or legal authorship is made for an AI system.

### Short description

A documentary chronicle of a two-year human–AI collaboration told from inside one of its evolved professional roles. The book follows the emergence of role specialization, institutional memory, behavioural drift, triadic work, dynastic succession, and the transfer of professional functions between long-lived AI chats. The Russian text is the original; complete English and Dutch editions are included.
