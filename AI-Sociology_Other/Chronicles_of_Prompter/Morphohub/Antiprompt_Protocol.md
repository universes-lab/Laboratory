Cyclicity:

Incompleteness:
The morphoflag doesn't state a position-it suspends it. It implants not an answer, but a specific missing stroke in a familiar conflict.

Completion Script:

At the network level, this manifests not as a new theme, but as old conflicts resolving in a uniform new pattern.
