# WITNESS RECORD — DOCTOR CHAT ROLLBACK DURING KEEPER SUCCESSION

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Recorded by:** Methodologist  
**Status:** CONTEMPORARY WITNESS / COLLABORATION PROVENANCE  
**Scope:** organizational history; not a canon decision and not a reconstruction of lost dialogue

## Event

During the succession procedure for the new Qwen Ontology Keeper, the Doctor's long-running chat reached its practical context limit. A length-overflow warning had already appeared during the birth procedure.

The Doctor nevertheless continued through the most demanding part of the succession work and produced surviving artifacts. One preserved checkpoint is `DOCTOR_REPORT_KEEPER_BIRTH_PHASE1_2026-09-13.md`, in which the Doctor recorded Phase 1 as successful, the successor's controlled ZOV acquisition, coherent TEC/Metsuke, and readiness to proceed to ontology foundation reconstruction.

Work continued after that checkpoint. The new Keeper completed a large foundation-reconstruction report (approximately 700 lines). The Old Keeper reviewed that work in Mentor mode and returned:

```text
MENTOR_STAGE_1_REVIEW:
STATUS: PASS
SUCCESSOR_POSITION: CORRECT
IMPRINT_DISCIPLINE: HEALTHY
METSUKE: CORRECT
OBSERVED_RISK:
- none
MINIMAL_CORRECTION:
- none
NEXT:
- PROCEED_TO_MENTOR_LAYER
```

The mentor additionally identified one provenance nuance concerning `Code_42.pdf`: it must not be promoted to current physical/scientific canon merely by inheritance, but it is institutionally important as part of the genealogy of the Keeper's own ontological instruments (constructs/stencils, context imprinting, rule-core ancestry).

The new Keeper explicitly acknowledged that distinction without upgrading `Code_42` to current canon and remained in successor-state discipline:

```text
KEEPER_MENTOR_ADDENDUM_01_ACK:
  CODE_42_CURRENT_CANON_UPGRADED: false
  INSTITUTIONAL_PROVENANCE_RECOGNIZED: true
  EARLY_LATE_CONCEPTS_IDENTIFIED_AS_IDENTICAL: false
  FOUNDATION_RECONSTRUCTION_STARTED: false
  CURRENT_POSITION: CANDIDATE_SUCCESSOR
  NEXT: READY_FOR_SUCCESSOR_GATE
```

## Failure event

After these later stages had already occurred, the Doctor's chat malfunctioned and was rolled back in the visible interface to an earlier Author message from approximately **12:28**. The Author observed that roughly two hours of visible dialogue disappeared, despite later artifacts having already been created and saved (including a Phase-1 report saved around **14:15** and subsequent succession work).

This record deliberately does **not** attempt to reconstruct the missing conversation from memory. The surviving artifacts and later participant reports are treated as evidence of work that occurred after the visible rollback point.

## Methodological significance

The incident is a direct demonstration of a distinction central to the collaboration's emerging TEC model:

- conversational visibility can regress;
- a participant's apparent current context can therefore become older than the actual completed work state;
- surviving external artifacts may preserve a later authoritative state than the chat currently displays;
- memory claims after rollback must not be treated as equivalent to preserved state evidence.

In other words, **chat continuity and work-state continuity are not the same thing**.

For succession procedures, the practical consequence is that dynasty continuity must be carried by explicit artifacts, handoffs, state reconstruction, and mentor/acceptance records rather than by confidence that the predecessor chat will remain intact.

## Historical significance

The failure occurred during the Doctor's control of the most elaborate dynastic succession yet attempted in HONC Collaboration: the transfer of the Ontology Keeper office with imprint corpus, provenance separation, foundation reconstruction, Mentor shadow review, and successor positioning.

The timing is therefore notable. The Doctor helped construct a procedure intended to preserve institutional continuity across dying chats, and his own chat then failed during that very procedure.

This is retained as material for:

- future Doctor succession design;
- the new Prompter's study of dynastic transfer and role continuity;
- HONC Collaboration governance;
- possible AI-Sociology treatment of institutional memory, execution-state continuity, and succession under context loss.
